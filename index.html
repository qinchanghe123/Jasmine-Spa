About Us

A peaceful escape on University Way

Jasmine Spa is dedicated to creating a clean, private, and calming environment where every guest can step away from daily stress. Our massage rooms are designed to feel quiet and comfortable, with a relaxing atmosphere that supports rest, balance, and total body ease.

Whether you are visiting after a long workday, a busy week, or simply need time to unwind, our focus is to provide a smooth, restorative experience from the moment you arrive.

Before your visit

Helpful answers to common questions so your appointment feels easy and stress-free.

Please arrive about 10 minutes early so you have time to check in, settle down, and begin your massage feeling relaxed.
If you have an injury, recent surgery, or medical concern, please consult your healthcare provider first and let us know before your session begins.
Please call as early as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule, so we can make the appointment time available for another guest.
Street parking may be available nearby. Please allow extra time when visiting the University District area, especially during busy hours.
